# Guide-to-Sessions Crosswalk

*How **How to Build a Course** (the Guide) maps onto the five pilot sessions, both directions. **If you're taking the pilot**, use Direction 1 to see which Guide sections back the session you just sat in, and Direction 2 to find where a section you're reading gets taught. **If you're working through the Guide on your own**, read Direction 1 as a suggested five-part sequence — it's the order the material is taught in when there's a room, and it works just as well alone.*

## Three things worth knowing

1. **The two councils are taught in different sessions on purpose.** The **Design** Council (four advisors who *want different things*, used *while you design* and cut) lands in Session 2, where you're deciding what stays. The **Review** Council (four *cooperative* reviewers of a *finished* draft) lands in Session 4, after there's something to review — and returns in Session 5 run on the whole course. Same idea, two moments; §6b names both.

2. **§7 Depth is taught three times, by design.** It shows up as *objective altitude* in Session 1 ("aim at the highest level the content honestly supports"), as *scope + time* in Session 2 ("depth drives time; time fits your container"), and as *trimming* in Session 3 ("one coherent capability per module"). Depth isn't a one-time decision — it's the recurring discipline that keeps scope honest.

3. **The seven practices recur across the whole build — they don't finish in Session 2.** Like depth (above), they're a recurring discipline, not a single step. You first meet them as a *move* in §3 (a **note → feature**, taught live with the extraction-map worked example, Session 2.3), but they come back at every scale:
   - **Course level — the audit (§3, Session 2.3):** run once against your objectives + Decisions, surfacing course-wide features.
   - **Per module — surface features (§3.5, Session 2.4):** run them again over *each module's* objectives on the **Decompose worksheet**, naming that module's features *before* the fit-check sizes them.
   - **Placed (§4, Session 2.5):** each feature lands in a module row of the MDD.
   - **Structured (§5, Session 3) → built (§6, Session 4):** you lay the features out in the three packages in §5, then *produce* them with GenAI in §6 — **Appendix D** gives the craft for each, and the teaching guide is written here, once the materials exist.
   - **Checked (§6b):** the **Review Council** scores it (Session 4; whole-course in Session 5).

   So the §3 audit is the *start* of a chain, not a one-off — and **Appendix D** is the bench reference you return to every time you actually build one of the features it named.
