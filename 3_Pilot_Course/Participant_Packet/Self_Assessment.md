# Self-Assessment — Can I Do These?

*Check yourself as you go. If you can't tick it, that's where to focus.*

## After Week 1 — Decisions & Objectives
- [ ] I have a one-page Decisions doc — **values**, audience, objective, depth, scope, tone, tools, and my **container** (session length x sessions = contact hours).
- [ ] I've filled the three that went home with me: **scaffolding form**, **who teaches it**, and **gates** (write "none" if your course has none).
- [ ] My objectives use observable Bloom verbs (no "understand/know").
- [ ] Each module objective ladders up to a course objective.
- [ ] I could write a quick check or task straight from each objective.

## After Week 2 — Master Design Document
- [ ] I ran the seven-practices audit and filled (or flagged) each one.
- [ ] I decomposed my course into right-sized modules that fit my time.
- [ ] My MDD has a course-header block + one table per module.
- [ ] My activities are **named and concrete**, not "students practice."
- [ ] I chose the ONE module I'll build.

## After Week 3 — Structure
- [ ] My module folder has all three packages laid out (skeleton).
- [ ] I planned the I-do → we-do → you-do scaffolding for each piece.
- [ ] I built in access across all three UDL principles (represent, engage, show mastery).
- [ ] I chose a module small enough to build to the floor.

## After Week 4 — Produce
- [ ] I built the complete solution first, then made practice by removing from it.
- [ ] I used GenAI with the guardrails and verified every line.
- [ ] I ran a Review Council review and revised.
- [ ] I kept an AI-direction + verification log (what I asked, what it got wrong, how I fixed it).

## After Week 5 — Teach-Back
- [ ] I built my module to the minimum-viable floor.
- [ ] I taught a 10-minute segment from my own teaching guide.
- [ ] **Portability:** someone else could teach my module from my materials alone.
- [ ] Any code/worked examples are correct and were verified.
- [ ] My module has a way for students to check themselves.
- [ ] I have a short plan to evaluate the course and improve it next run.

## The deliverables
- [ ] **A** — Objectives
- [ ] **B** — Master Design Document
- [ ] **C** — One complete module, built and taught
