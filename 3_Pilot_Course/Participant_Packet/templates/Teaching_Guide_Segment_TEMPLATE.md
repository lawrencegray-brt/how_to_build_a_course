*Add the cues your medium needs. A shop floor wants `[DEMO at machine]`, `[WALK to station]`, `[HAND OUT]`, `[SAFETY CHECK]`; a discussion wants `[HARVEST answers]`, `[SILENCE — count ten]`. The list above is screen-shaped because most courses are; yours may not be.*

**If this segment is a discussion**, the script is less about what you say and more about what you do with what they say. Four moves worth writing down in advance:

- **Harvest in their words.** Write answers on the board as they said them, not as you'd have phrased them. Rewording on the spot tells the room their version was wrong.
- **Decide beforehand whether this segment converges.** Some discussions end in one answer; some end in a well-mapped disagreement. Both are fine — but you have to know which you're running, or you'll cut it off at the wrong moment.
- **Count ten seconds of silence.** It will feel like a minute. The second half of the wait is where the quieter people speak.
- **For a plausible-but-wrong answer:** "say more about that" — it surfaces the reasoning, which is the thing that actually needs correcting. **For the one loud voice:** "let's hear two more before we judge."
