# Pre-Class — Session 1 (Decide & Write Objectives)

*~20 minutes. The goal is to arrive ready to work, not to learn it all in advance.*

## Do this before Session 1
1. **Decide your subject.** Pick one thing you know well and want to teach. Bring it — it's your running project for all five weeks.
2. **Skim the guide, Sections 0–2** (`How_To_Build_A_Course_GUIDE`): the big picture, "decisions before production," and writing objectives. Don't study — just get the shape.
3. **Glance at one worked example's Decisions doc** — `2_Worked_Examples/IntroML/01_Decisions.md` or `2_Worked_Examples/Coffee/01_Decisions.md`. Notice it's one page.

## Come ready to answer
- Who is your course *for*, and what should they be able to *do* at the end?

*That's it. We build your Decisions doc and objectives live.*
