# Pre-Class — Session 5 (Teach-Back & Feedback)

*Time varies — this is the build week (plan for ~6–10 focused hours).*

## Do this before Session 5
1. **Build your one complete module to the minimum-viable floor** — all three packages (instructor materials, student materials, teaching guide). Use `Module_Skeleton_TEMPLATE/` and copy from a worked example.
2. **Run a Review Council review** on your module (personas: SME, student, instructional designer, professor) — as one chat prompt (Guide §10) or as four *separate* agents reading the module folder (Guide §6e). Score it on consistency, delivery, factuality, and teaching principles; **revise** based on what it finds. Keep the **AI-direction + verification log** (template in your fill-in file) — what you asked for, what it got wrong or said it *could not verify*, how you caught and fixed it.
3. **Prepare a 10-minute teach-back** of ONE segment — teach it from your own teaching guide (floor or gold tier, your call).
4. **Re-read the module rubric** (in the syllabus) — your teach-back is checked against it, especially *portability*: could someone teach your module from your guide alone?

## Come ready to
- Teach your 10 minutes, and give specific, kind feedback to your peers.
