# Pre-Class — Session 2 (Master Design Document)

*~30 minutes.*

## Do this before Session 2
1. **Finish your Decisions doc and objectives** (Deliverable A) if you haven't.
2. **Read the guide, Section 3 (Seven Practices)** and **Section 3.5 (Decompose Into Modules)**. Skim **Section 4 (the MDD)**.
3. **Run the Seven-Practices audit on your own course** — use `templates/Seven_Practices_Audit_TEMPLATE.md`. For each practice, write your answer as a concrete feature. Where you have no answer, that's a gap to bring to class. **Do your own pass first;** then, if you like, put an LLM to work — ask it to *fill* the practices you blanked on (five candidate features each) and to *widen* ("what am I missing?"). Keep only what fits your audience and rewrite it in your voice. *(Unsure how to build a good feature? The guide's **Appendix D** has a craft note for each of the seven.)*
4. **Glance at a filled MDD** — `2_Worked_Examples/Coffee/04_MDD.md` — to see the format.

## Come ready to
- Share your objectives for feedback, and bring your practices audit (gaps included).
