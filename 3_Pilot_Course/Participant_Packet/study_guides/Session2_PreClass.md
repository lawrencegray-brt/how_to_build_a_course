# Pre-Class — Session 2 (Master Design Document)

*~30 minutes.*

## Do this before Session 2
1. **Finish your Decisions doc and objectives** (Deliverable A) if you haven't.
2. **Check your objectives — this is the objectives feedback.** There is no feedback round in class, so this step has to happen before you walk in. First check each course objective yourself against **Rubric 5.1** in your packet: a measurable verb · student-focused · the right altitude · assessable. (*Laddered*, the fifth line, waits until you have module objectives.) Then run the three prompts from Session 1, all on the **Directing AI** card by id: **AI-2.1 CHECK** the four traps (*"…or 'none'. Do not rewrite them."*), **AI-2.2 WIDEN** (*"what would a student in a course like this usually be able to do that none of my objectives names? Give each as a measurable objective."*), **AI-2.3 SIMULATE** (*"write three things a student might hand in as evidence they met it"*). Fix what you agree with. **Bring what you disagreed with** — there is a question slot at the end of class, and those are the interesting ones.
3. **Read the guide, Section 3 (Seven Practices)** and **Section 3.5 (Decompose Into Modules)**. Skim **Section 4 (the MDD)**.
4. **Run the Seven-Practices audit on your own course** — use `templates/Seven_Practices_Audit_TEMPLATE.md`. For each practice, write your answer as a concrete feature. Where you have no answer, that's a gap to bring to class. **Do your own pass first;** then, if you like, put an LLM to work on the practices you blanked on — **AI-3.1** on the Directing AI card: your Decisions doc and objectives in, features that serve *those* objectives out — and *widen* ("given these objectives and this audience, what am I still missing?"). Keep only what fits your audience and rewrite it in your voice. *(Unsure how to build a good feature? The guide's **Appendix D** has a craft note for each of the seven.)*
5. **Glance at the filled coffee pair** — `2_Worked_Examples/Coffee/03b_Decompose.md` and `04_MDD.md`. In class you will see both walked step by step. **You build your own during the week after class, not in class** — decomposing a course and writing its design document take real thought, and the finished example is there to work beside.
