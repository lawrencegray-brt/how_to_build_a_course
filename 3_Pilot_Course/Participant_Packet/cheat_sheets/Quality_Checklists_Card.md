# Quality Checklists — Quick Card

*The three "is this good enough yet?" checks, in the order you'll need them. Run each one **before** you move on — a gap caught here is minutes; the same gap caught in Week 5 is a rebuild. (These also live in Guide §10.)*

---

## Every objective

Run this on each objective as you write it — course-level and module-level alike.

- [ ] **Measurable verb?** You can watch a student do it. Not *understand, know, learn, appreciate, be familiar with, grasp.*
- [ ] **Student-focused?** It says what the *student* will do, not what you will cover.
- [ ] **Right altitude?** The Bloom level matches what they actually need — higher isn't automatically better.
- [ ] **Laddered to a course objective?** Every module objective is tagged →CO#.
- [ ] **Assessable?** You can name, right now, the thing a student would produce to prove it.

> If an objective fails the first box, fix it before anything else. Every later artifact inherits the verb.

---

## Your MDD

Run this once the whole blueprint is drafted, before you build anything.

- [ ] **Every module has objectives / topics / materials / activities?** No blank cells.
- [ ] **Objectives tagged →CO#?** Anything that traces to nothing is either scope creep to cut, or an objective you forgot to write.
- [ ] **Activities named, not vague?** "Students practice" is not buildable. *"Pair exercise: fill-in-the-blank, ~70% provided"* is.
- [ ] **Practice-before-assessment present?** Nobody is assessed on something they haven't rehearsed.
- [ ] **Values shown?** Mapped to specific activities, not restated as a list.

---

## One module

Run this before you call a module done.

- [ ] **All three packages present?** Instructor materials, student materials, teaching guide.
- [ ] **Portable — could someone *else* teach it from the guide alone, cold?** This is the real bar, not "could I teach it."
- [ ] **Scaffolded, not blank?** Practice has fill-ins, TODOs or partial support — never an empty page.
- [ ] **Correct / verified?** You ran it, did it, or checked it yourself. "The AI wrote it" is not a verification.
- [ ] **Visual where it helps?** Paired with text, never replacing it.
- [ ] **A way for students to self-check?** They can tell whether they got it right without you.

---

### Two things these checklists assume

**You pass your own self-check first.** Writing a module and being able to teach it are different capabilities. Take the self-check yourself — at your desk, where a gap costs ten minutes.

**Teach what you assess, with no exceptions.** Walk each checklist item back to the moment it's taught. Assessing something the course never taught feels like a trap to the student, and it's the one error they won't forgive.
