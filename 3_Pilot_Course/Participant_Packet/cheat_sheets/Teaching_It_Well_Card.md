# Teaching It Well — Eight Principles

*The method builds a course anyone can teach; these are how you make it **unforgettable**. Apply them when you teach your module back — and in every course you deliver. (Full version: Guide, "Teaching It Well.")*

1. **Show the whole picture first.** Give the map before the details — every piece then has a place to land.
2. **Break it into small, sure steps.** Pieces small enough to feel doable; confidence compounds.
3. **Make it land — an analogy for clarity, a story for memory.** Both in your own voice, never borrowed.
4. **Draw it live; make it interactive.** Build the diagram in front of them and narrate as you go; favor interactive visuals over static ones.
5. **Go slow, and spend energy where the content earns it.** A deliberate pace; lift your energy for what matters.
6. **Teach warm — be the first to say "I didn't get this either."** Vulnerability makes not-knowing safe — the only place learning starts.
7. **Ask, don't just tell.** Questions that get at the heart of an idea; a question they answer sticks.
8. **Leave them wanting the next room.** Open loops; make the subject feel bigger than the session.
