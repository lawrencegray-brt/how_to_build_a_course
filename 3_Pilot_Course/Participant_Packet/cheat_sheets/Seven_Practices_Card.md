# Seven Practices — Quick Card

*Audit your design. For each, answer the question **as a concrete feature you'll build.** No answer = your next design task. (Full question bank: Guide Appendix B.)*

1. **Activate Prior Learning** — What analogy connects what they already know to this?
2. **Organize Knowledge** — What's the relationship among the key concepts? (Can you draw a map?)
3. **Motivate** — What excites you about this? What would excite them?
4. **Develop Mastery** — What model shows the performance level to aim at?
5. **Practice & Feedback** — How do they practice *before* being assessed?
6. **Student Development & Climate** — What traits does a strong practitioner have — and does the course cultivate them?
7. **Self-Directed Learners** — What lets them keep learning after the course ends?
