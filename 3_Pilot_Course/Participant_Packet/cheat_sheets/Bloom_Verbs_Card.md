# Bloom's Verbs — Quick Card

*Pick the verb that matches what students should actually do. Aim high where the content supports it; lower levels are fine when the goal sits there.*

| Level | Use for | Verbs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Remember** | recall facts | define, list, name, recall, label, identify |
| **Understand** | explain ideas | summarize, explain, interpret, describe, classify, compare |
| **Apply** | use in new situations | apply, build, solve, use, implement, demonstrate |
| **Analyze** | break into parts | analyze, distinguish, examine, compare, differentiate |
| **Evaluate** | judge against criteria | assess, justify, critique, recommend, defend, rate |
| **Create** | make something new | design, develop, compose, propose, formulate, construct |

**Avoid (not observable):** understand, know, learn, appreciate, be familiar with, grasp — *as the objective verb.* Replace with one above.

**Test:** could you *watch* a student do it? If not, rewrite.
