## Course-level objectives
By the end of the course, students will be able to:

1. **([Bloom verb])** … — *Evidence (how they'll prove it):* …
2. **([Bloom verb])** … — *Evidence:* …
3. **([Bloom verb])** … — *Evidence:* …
4. **([Bloom verb])** … — *Evidence:* …
5. **([Bloom verb])** … — *Evidence:* …

*The **Evidence** is the Backward-Design proof (Guide §3.5, Step 1): the task, artifact, or performance that shows the objective is met — not necessarily a grade. You add it here when you decompose; it's what every module builds toward, and it feeds the MDD's assessment/activities later.*
