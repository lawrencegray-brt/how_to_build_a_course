# The Method on a Page

*A quick-reference to keep beside you — shorthand for once you know the method. New here? Read **Start Here** and the Guide first; this page won't make sense cold.*

**The throughline:**
> Decisions (values first) → Bloom objectives → seven practices → Master Design Document → the three-package module → produce with GenAI under guardrails.

**The one rule:** Decide before you produce.

## The steps
*(Step numbers are the conceptual order; the guide section is noted in parentheses.)*

1. **Decisions** (§1) — one page: audience, objective, depth, scope, tone, tools.
2. **Objectives** (§2) — what the student can *do*; Bloom verbs; course + module (laddered).
3. **Seven practices** (§3) — audit the design; each answer becomes a feature.
4. **Decompose** (§3.5) — break into right-sized modules that fit your contact hours.
5. **MDD** (§4) — course-header block + one table per module (objectives, topics, materials, activities).
6. **Three packages** (§5) — instructor materials · student materials · teaching guide.
7. **Produce with GenAI** (§6) — persona → solution-first → degrade to practice → ground → verify. You decide; it drafts; you check everything.
8. **Set depth** (§7) — only as deep as the goal needs. The **Review Council** (§6b) reviews each module.

**Across it all:** design for all learners — accessibility / UDL (§8).
**After you teach:** evaluate & improve — close the loop (§9).

## The deliverables (what you'll have at the end)
- **A** — Objectives  ·  **B** — Master Design Document  ·  **C** — one module, built & taught.

*(In the pilot course these are Deliverables A, B and C. Building on your own, they're simply the three things you finish with.)*

## The tests
- **Measurable:** can you observe the student doing it?
- **Portable:** could someone else teach this from your materials?
