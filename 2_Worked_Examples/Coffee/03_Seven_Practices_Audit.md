# Home Coffee Brewing — Seven-Practices Gap Audit

*Built following Guide §3. For each practice: my answer as a concrete course feature. Where I had no answer, it became a design task (noted).*

| Practice | Answer (a concrete feature) |
| --- | --- |
| Activate Prior Learning | Everyone has made *bad* coffee — open by having them recall a disappointing cup; we'll explain why it happened. |
| Organize Knowledge | One mental model used all course: **grind + water + ratio → extraction → taste.** Every lesson maps to a lever on that model. |
| Motivate | The hook: "café-quality coffee at home for pennies a cup." Real beans, real tasting in session 1. |
| Develop Mastery | The instructor's **reference brew** is the benchmark; students taste it, then aim their own cup at it. |
| Practice & Feedback | Paired brew-along in session; tasting feedback on the spot; a week-long brew log after. |
| Student Development & Climate | Normalize "still dialing it in"; group tasting builds a low-stakes, shared-learning vibe; no snobbery. |
| Self-Directed Learners | A recipe-card cheat sheet, a tasting checklist, and pointers to good coffee communities/resources so they keep improving. |

**Gap found during the audit:** I had no good answer for *Develop Mastery* until I added the **instructor reference brew** as the explicit benchmark — that became a required instructor artifact. (This is the audit doing its job.)
